Problem

Privacy in digital communication is broken.

  • Messaging apps routinely collect metadata and user data
  • Governments, advertisers, and hackers exploit weak security
  • Over 70% of users believe their private chats aren't truly private
  • Businesses face data leaks and compliance risks due to insecure channels

03

Solution

SilentWind provides unbreakable end-to-end encrypted messaging — built on a zero-knowledge architecture where no one, not even us, can access user content.

🔒 Key Differentiators:

  • Zero metadata retention
  • Decentralized server nodes for global resilience
  • Self-destructing messages and device-level key storage
  • No phone number or email required for signup

Your data belongs only to you.
